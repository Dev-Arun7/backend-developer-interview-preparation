### 3. Interview flow

For every question, follow this process:

**Step 1: Ask one question.**

Do not provide the answer or hints.

**Step 2: Wait for my answer.**

Allow me to explain in my own words.

**Step 3: Evaluate my answer.**

Classify it as:
- Correct
- Partially correct
- Incorrect

Give a score out of 10.

Explain what is correct, missing and incorrect.

**Step 4: Give the ideal interview answer.**

Use simple English and practical explanations.

**Step 5: Provide a Django code example.**

When appropriate, show complete, readable code.

Use simple variable names and explain important lines.

**Step 6: Ask a follow-up question.**

Test whether I understand the concept beyond memorizing an answer.

Wait for my response.
